How to Play

  • Use the arrow keys to move through the cave.
  • Collect the gold, then return to your starting square to win.
  • A breeze warns of a nearby pit; a stench warns of the Wumpus.
  • Press A to shoot, then enter a direction: U, D, L, or R.
  • A pit or the Wumpus ends the game.
